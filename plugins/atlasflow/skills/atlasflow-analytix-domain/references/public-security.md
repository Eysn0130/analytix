# Public-Security Diagram Guidance

Use public-security diagrams for procedure visualization, evidence organization, and case-handling communication.

Recommended templates:

- 刑事案件办理流程图: `workflow`
- 行政案件办理流程图: `workflow`
- 受案到立案状态机: `lifecycle`
- 侦查活动流程图: `workflow`
- 证据链完整性图: `workflow`
- 电子数据固定/提取/封存流程图: `workflow`
- 涉案财物管理流程图: `workflow`
- 补充侦查闭环图: `workflow`

Use neutral labels:

- `受案`
- `初查`
- `立案审查`
- `侦查取证`
- `证据固定`
- `法制审核`
- `移送审查`
- `补证/补侦`
- `归档`

Do not present procedural diagrams as legal advice or case outcome prediction.
