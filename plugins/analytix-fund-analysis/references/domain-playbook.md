# Domain Playbook

This reference keeps user-facing economic-investigation wording compact and evidence-oriented.

## Account And Fund Features

- `账户快进快出可疑特征`: funds enter and leave quickly. Support with holding time, outbound ratio, balance retention, related counterparties, and time windows.
- `短时过账特征`: rapid pass-through behavior. Use when evidence is not enough to classify account role.
- `分散转入、集中转出特征`: many sources converge then leave to few destinations.
- `集中转入、分散转出特征`: few sources feed many destinations.
- `资金归集/沉淀账户特征`: account gathers or retains funds. Prefer this wording over casual “资金池”.
- `个人账户经营性使用特征`: personal account shows business-settlement behavior. Do not call it tax evasion without tax, invoice, contract, and ledger evidence.
- `生活消费账户特征`: salary/living expenses/platform consumption dominate and fund-flow suspiciousness is lower.
- `三方支付通道出入金线索`: payment institutions appear as channel or settlement path; do not assume platform account ownership from keyword alone.
- `虚拟资产购置或出入金线索`: requires strong exchange, OTC, wallet, token, or payment-chain evidence.
- `代持或实际控制线索`: combine identity/contact/device overlap with fund behavior.
- `交易环境关联线索`: shared IP, MAC, teller, branch, location, merchant, receipt, or terminal fields. This is a correlation lead, not proof of the same operator or controller.
- `团伙/共同控制关联线索`: multiple independent overlaps across accounts, counterparties, contact/address, device/IP/MAC, merchants, or synchronized windows. Do not call it gang/co-offending without external evidence.
- `票税合同线索`: invoice, tax, contract, logistics, service, acceptance, or ledger clues around company loops and public-to-private flows. Do not call it false invoicing or tax evasion without documents and tax/ledger proof.

## Transaction Features

- 大额交易: define threshold and count/amount/time scope.
- 夜间交易: define clock window.
- 临界金额拆分: repeated amounts just below a threshold; needs parameterized threshold.
- 高频小额: many low-value transactions in a short period.
- 资金回流/闭环流转: funds return to origin or related account through one or more hops.
- 交易对手信息缺失: report as data/evidence gap, not as a real counterparty.

## Asset And Debt Leads

Look for vehicle, real estate, renovation, property fee, parking fee, wealth management, securities, insurance, loan disbursement, repayment, guarantee, enforcement deduction, and litigation-related flows. Payment rows can indicate a lead, but ownership or legal effect requires external evidence.

## Wording Rules

- Use `可疑特征`, `线索`, `需复核`, `待补证`.
- Avoid `构成犯罪`, `偷逃税成立`, `非法获利`, `实际所有`, or `洗钱成立`.
- Bank-flow analysis can support investigative leads; it does not replace legal, tax, asset-registry, invoice, contract, or interrogation evidence.
