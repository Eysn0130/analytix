## Why

Instruction overlap and fixed review/report rituals can slow accepted Analytix delivery without increasing evidence quality. The user approved the instruction audit and requested a model-neutral, efficient RC workflow while preserving complete requirements and real approval boundaries.

## What Changes

- Make task briefs and report artifacts proportional to ownership, risk and recovery needs.
- Clarify direct work versus commissioned fleet control and preserve main-agent synthesis.
- Bind reusable verification to the actual candidate and environment; remove repeated checks with no changed decision.
- Keep OpenSpec authoritative for absorption requirements and Superpowers reference-only unless separately admitted.
- Align project, personal and installed skill guidance; retain recoverable local maintenance records outside the product source.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `agent-handoff-review`: proportional briefs/evidence, scope-aware autonomy and candidate-bound verification without weakening admission or accepted outcomes.

## Impact

Repository guidance, canonical OpenSpec skills and client adapters, and the RC skill/protocol. No Analytix production code, user data, runtime schema, dependency or release policy changes. Host skill/config maintenance remains outside the product artifact; this change does not install Superpowers hooks or add tools.
