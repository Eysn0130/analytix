## Contract used by every migrated row

- Renderer, Main, settings, threads, schedules, messages, jobs, and subagent
  records may carry only key-free user intent such as `providerId`, `model`,
  `reasoningEffort`, mode, prompt, and workspace.
- `packages/runtime-go` resolves the selected committed Registry winner under
  the Go effect boundary. Key-free step intent may perform a bounded
  availability read solely to choose the committed route-pool winner and clears
  it before returning; each physical Provider attempt then re-resolves its own
  endpoint, proxy, model, and credential. The proxy is process-local attempt
  authority and is used only to construct that Provider transport; neither
  proxy nor credential material is durable or public.
- Every model step refreshes key-free Registry intent; every prepared attempt
  resolves current Registry execution again. The exact process-local authority
  is revalidated immediately before every physical send, including internal
  stream reconnects, and again after the response. Drift before send reaches no
  transport; drift after send discards the response before public or durable
  consumption. A composition that supports late-bound intent but omits that
  validator fails closed.
- `migrated`, `readiness-only`, and `absent` are implementation
  classifications, not release claims.

## Executable closure

`scripts/verify-local-provider-consumers.mjs` checks executable capabilities,
not inventory names. It scans production Main TypeScript for unallowlisted
Provider-like network effects, rejects direct Provider capability in the two
formerly direct ordinary consumers, rejects copied Provider launch argv,
requires ambient Provider env stripping, and requires the Go intent/effect/
current per-send pre-send and post-response bracket, current per-attempt proxy
transport, fail-closed validator
composition, plus credential/proxy clearing in pending work, the private Go
media JIT, proxy, redirect, download, Vision Bridge, and currentness owners.
Explicit tests, test-support compatibility clients, readiness-only owners, and
K8 lifecycle owners are bounded exceptions; production media owners are no
longer allowlisted.
