## Pinned tool-authority comparison — 2026-07-20

The source review at the pin above identified useful donors: a turn-frozen tool
catalog, execution-time policy re-resolution, non-mutating Ajv schema validation,
MCP search/describe/call, and avoidance of blind replay after an unknown outcome.
It did not prove Analytix parity or execution of the complete Kun test suite.

Current requirements have independent owners:

- [Case evidence kernel](../../../openspec/specs/case-evidence-kernel/spec.md):
  immutable host-derived turn context and exact current execution grants.
- [MCP readiness](../../../openspec/specs/case-source-mcp-readiness/spec.md):
  advertised/executed allowlist identity, schema validation and semantic outcomes.
- [Publication gate](../../../openspec/specs/case-claim-publication-gate/spec.md):
  final evidence and recovery boundaries, ephemeral reasoning, and the separately
  scoped protected process-local native display exception. The old ledger does
  not override that exception or authorize generic/durable reasoning exposure.
- [Agent foundation](../../../openspec/specs/agent-platform-foundation/spec.md):
  one Go Harness and optional-plugin fault isolation. Case-specific authority
  requirements do not turn the old Kun audit into an ordinary-startup blocker.
- [Benchmark method](../specs/09-agent-quality-product-benchmark.md) and
  [admission rules](README.md): claims require scoped fresh evidence and valid
  provenance. The review marker remains `not-proven`.
